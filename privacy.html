GTA Rush Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

"GTA Rush Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the GTA Rush YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos on that one channel and to read that channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • YouTube API Services, through Google OAuth, with the owner's consent: upload videos and set their metadata on the GTA Rush channel (youtube.upload), read the channel identity to confirm it is GTA Rush (youtube.readonly), and read that channel's own analytics (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • No data about other users or viewers is collected, sold or shared.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by that user. Nothing is sent to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the tool is used. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local files are then deleted.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact
